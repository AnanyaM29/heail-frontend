Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

HEAIL ("HEAIL", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through heail.in and our related services (together, the "Services"), why we collect it, how it is used and protected, and the rights you have over it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. In this policy, "you" or "Data Principal" means any individual whose personal data we process — including registered users, employees and candidates enrolled by an organisation, and visitors to our website.

1. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with HEAIL, we collect:

  • Account & contact data — name, email address, mobile number, city, and country, collected at registration and OTP-verified for email.
  • Organisation data — organisation name, industry, and size, where you register as an organisation admin.
  • Employee/candidate data — name, email, mobile (optional), and level, submitted by an organisation admin to enrol employees, or by an HR user to invite candidates.
  • Assessment data — your answers, scores, and completion status for each assessment you take.
  • Payment data — the amount, currency, GST breakdown, and a payment reference for each order. Card, bank, and PayPal account details are handled entirely by PayPal; HEAIL never receives or stores them.
  • Usage & technical data — log-in timestamps, and, only where you consent via the cookie banner, optional analytics cookies.
2. Why We Process Your Data

We process personal data only for specified, lawful purposes, including to:

  • Create and authenticate your account, and verify you by one-time code.
  • Generate, deliver, and score the assessments you or your organisation have purchased.
  • Send transactional communications — invitations, reminders, payment confirmations, invoices, and result notifications.
  • Produce aggregate, non-identifying reports for organisation admins (individual answers and scores are never included).
  • Process payments and issue GST-compliant invoices.
  • Comply with applicable law, and respond to lawful requests from public authorities.
3. Consent

Where consent is the basis for processing, we ask for it in clear language before it is relied upon — for example, the Terms of Agreement you accept before creating an account and before each payment, and the cookie banner for optional analytics cookies, which offers an equally prominent "Reject non-essential" option. Every acceptance is logged with its version and timestamp. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us at the contact address below, with the same ease with which it was given; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, or your obligations under any purchase already completed.

4. Confidentiality of Assessment Results

If you take an assessment because your organisation enrolled you, your individual answers and scores are confidential and are never shared with your organisation, your manager, or any hiring team — this is enforced at the platform level. Your organisation receives only aggregate, RAG-banded results across a group of respondents, and only once a minimum-respondent threshold is met.

5. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary to operate the Services:

  • With PayPal, to process payments — limited to what is required to complete a transaction.
  • With our email and hosting infrastructure providers, solely to deliver the Services (e.g. sending you an assessment link).
  • With the organisation that purchased a diagnostic for you, but only aggregate results as described in Section 4 — never your individual answers or score.
  • Where required by law, regulation, or a valid order of a court or government authority.
6. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet accounting, tax, dispute-resolution, and legal obligations (including invoices, which are retained per applicable GST record-keeping requirements). Assessment session and result history is retained so that leaders can see their attempt history over time. Where you ask us to erase your data under Section 7 and no legal basis requires continued retention, we will delete or irreversibly anonymise it.

7. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is being processed.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Erase personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as described in Section 3.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint about how your data has been handled, as described below.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at the contact address below. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Rules.

8. Data Security

We apply technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold, including encrypted password storage, encrypted connections (TLS) between your browser and our servers, rate-limiting on OTP and authentication endpoints, and access controls that enforce the confidentiality described in Section 4 at the application level — not merely by policy.

9. Cookies

HEAIL uses essential cookies required to keep you signed in and to operate the site securely. Optional analytics cookies are used only if you accept them via the cookie banner shown on your first visit; you may decline them and still use the full functionality of the Services.

10. Children's Data

The Services are not directed at children, except where a future Student Solutions offering is introduced — at which point this policy will be updated with the specific safeguards and verifiable-parental-consent mechanism required under the DPDP Act before that offering goes live. Today, no HEAIL product is designed to knowingly collect personal data from a child.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; material changes will be reflected by a new "Last updated" date above.

12. Grievance Officer & Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy, to exercise your rights as a Data Principal, or to raise a grievance about how your personal data has been handled, contact our Grievance Officer at contact@heail.in. We aim to acknowledge grievances promptly and resolve them within the timeframe prescribed under the DPDP Act and Rules.
