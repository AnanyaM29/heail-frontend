Inside the Bottlenecks

The challenges every organisation will recognise.

People

The right people in the wrong seats. Capable individuals underperform because roles, strengths and expectations were never honestly matched.
Quiet disengagement. People stopped trying long before they stop showing up.
Attrition treated as weather. Exits are processed, not studied.
Skills assumed, never verified. Promotions run on reputation rather than measured capability.

HR

Systems built for compliance, not growth. HR processes prove that rules were followed — not that people improved.
Hiring by impression. Interviews reward confidence; the real requirement is rarely measured against.
Appraisals nobody believes. Annual reviews that neither side trusts.
Training without evidence. Programs are attended, rated "good" — and nobody can show what changed.

Leadership

The belief gap. What leadership believes and what employees experience are two different companies.
Decisions by hierarchy, not evidence. The loudest voice wins; data attends the meeting but never votes.
Untested self-awareness. Leaders have never seen their own leadership measured.
Fear disguised as harmony. Bad news travels upward slowly or not at all.

Growth

Strategy that lives in a slide deck. The plan exists; daily decisions ignore it.
Growth that outruns structure. Revenue scales; coordination and culture don't.
Customer experience without an inside mirror. External promises made by teams whose internal friction is unmeasured.
Future-readiness by assumption. No one has measured whether the organisation can absorb change.
