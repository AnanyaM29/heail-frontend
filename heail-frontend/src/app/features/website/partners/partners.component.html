Partners

Grow with HEAIL in your city.

Consultants, trainers, coaches, and HR professionals: bring HEAIL's measurement-first transformation to the organisations and leaders around you — with our platform, our methodology, and our support behind you.

What partners do

  • Introduce HEAIL solutions to organisations and leaders in their city
  • Participate in local delivery of the 1-Year Leadership and Five-Layer Transformation programs — managing engagements locally and learning the HEAIL methodology from inside
  • Grow into certified facilitators of HEAIL debriefs and workshops

What partners receive

  • The HEAIL partner kit and methodology orientation
  • Demo access — one Pulse and one Gita Leader assessment, on us
  • A share in the value of every engagement they bring and help deliver
  • A front seat in a growing transformation practice
